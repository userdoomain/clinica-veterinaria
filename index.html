Still have questions?

Here are answers to the questions we get most often.

Do I need to book an appointment or can I just bring my pet in?

Routine consultations are by appointment to secure the best time for you. Urgent and emergency cases are seen immediately, 24 hours a day, with no appointment needed.

Which species do you treat?

We treat dogs and cats across all our services, including surgery, hospitalisation and ICU. For other species such as birds and small rodents, we can refer you to specialist partners.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes, we do home visits for pets with mobility issues or elderly owners. Book by phone or WhatsApp — the travel fee is confirmed when you book.

How does the vaccination plan work?

At the first appointment we review your pet's history and build a personalised schedule. For puppies, the protocol includes several V8/V10 doses at set intervals, plus an annual rabies vaccine.

Do you offer instalments and do you accept pet insurance?

We accept credit cards in up to 6 instalments, bank transfer (PIX), cash and partner pet insurance plans. For higher-value procedures we provide a detailed written estimate before any work begins.
